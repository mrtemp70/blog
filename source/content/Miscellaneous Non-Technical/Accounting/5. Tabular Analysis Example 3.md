# P1-4A: Beckham Deliveries (Tabular Analysis)

## Problem Statement
**Gordon Beckham** started his own delivery service, **Beckham Deliveries**, on June 1, 2012. The following transactions occurred during the month of June:

* **June 1:** Gordon invested $10,000 cash in the business.
* **June 2:** Purchased a used van for deliveries for $12,000. Gordon paid $2,000 cash and signed a note payable for the remaining balance ($10,000).
* **June 3:** Paid $500 for office rent for the month.
* **June 5:** Performed $4,400 of services on account.
* **June 9:** Withdrew $200 cash for personal use.
* **June 12:** Purchased supplies for $150 on account.
* **June 15:** Received a cash payment of $1,250 for services provided on June 5.
* **June 17:** Purchased gasoline for $200 on account.
* **June 20:** Received a cash payment of $1,300 for services provided.
* **June 23:** Made a cash payment of $600 on the note payable.
* **June 26:** Paid $250 for utilities.
* **June 29:** Paid for the gasoline purchased on account on June 17 ($200).
* **June 30:** Paid $1,000 for employee salaries.

### Accounting Equation Verification

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Total Assets} &= \text{Cash} + \text{Accounts Receivable} + \text{Supplies} + \text{Equipment} \\
&= 7,500 + 3,150 + 150 + 12,000 \\
&= \mathbf{\$22,800}
\end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Total Liabilities \& Equity} &= \text{Notes Payable} + \text{Accounts Payable} + \text{Capital} - \text{Drawings} + \text{Revenue} - \text{Expenses} \\
&= 9,400 + 150 + 10,000 - 200 + 5,700 - 1,950 \\
&= \mathbf{\$22,800}
\end{aligned}$$

$$\mathbf{\$22,800 = \$22,800 \quad \checkmark \text{ (Balanced)}}$$
