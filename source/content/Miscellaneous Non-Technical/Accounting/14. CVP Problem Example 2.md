## Problem Statement
The company's quarterly contribution format income statement is as follows:

| Particulars | Total ($) | Per Unit ($) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Sales | 450,000 | 30 |
| Less: Variable expenses | 180,000 | 12 |
| **Contribution Margin** | **270,000** | **18** |
| Less: Fixed expenses | 216,000 | |
| **Net Operating Income** | **54,000** | |
## Requirements / Questions

1. What is the quarterly break-even point in units and in sales dollars?
2. Without resorting to computations, what is the total contribution margin at the break-even point?
3. How many units would have to be sold each quarter to earn a target profit of $90,000? Use the contribution margin method. Verify your answer by preparing a contribution format income statement at the target sales level.
4. Refer to the original data. Compute the company's margin of safety in both dollar and percentage terms.
5. What is the company's CM ratio? If sales increase by $50,000 per quarter & there is no change in fixed expenses, by how much would you expect quarterly net operating income to increase?

---

**Answer:**

## 📐 Solution 1: Break-Even Point (B/E Point)

### A. Break-Even Point in Units:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{B/E Point (Units)} &= \frac{\text{Total Fixed Cost (TFC)}}{\text{CM Per Unit (CM P/U)}} \\
&= \frac{\$216,000}{\$18} \\
&= \mathbf{12,000 \text{ Units}}
\end{aligned}$$

### B. Break-Even Point in Sales Dollars:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{B/E Point (Dollars)} &= \text{B/E Point (Units)} \times \text{Selling Price Per Unit} \\
&= 12,000 \times \$30 \\
&= \mathbf{\$360,000 \text{ Dollars}}
\end{aligned}$$

---

## 🧠 Solution 2: CM at Break-Even Point (Without Computation)

* **Answer:** **$216,000**
* **Explanation (বাংলায় ব্যাখ্যা):** সমচ্ছেদ বিন্দু বা Break-Even Point-এর মূল শর্তই হলো ব্যবসায় কোনো লাভ বা লোকসান থাকবে না $(\text{Profit} = 0)$। যেহেতু লাভ বা ক্ষতি কিছুই নেই, তাই কোম্পানির মোট কন্ট্রিবিউশন মার্জিন (CM) ঠিক তার মোট স্থায়ী খরচ বা Fixed Cost-এর সমান হবে। অর্থাৎ, $\text{Total CM} = \text{Total Fixed Cost} = \mathbf{\$216,000}$।

---

## 🎯 Solution 3: Target Profit Analysis ($90,000 Profit)

### A. Unit Sales to Earn Target Profit:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Required Unit Sales} &= \frac{\text{TFC} + \text{Target Profit (TP)}}{\text{CM Per Unit (CM P/U)}} \\
&= \frac{\$216,000 + \$90,000}{\$18} \\
&= \frac{\$306,000}{\$18} \\
&= \mathbf{17,000 \text{ Units}}
\end{aligned}$$

### B. Verification Income Statement (17,000 Units Level):
$$\text{Total Sales} = 17,000 \times \$30 = \$510,000$$
$$\text{Total Variable Expenses} = 17,000 \times \$12 = \$204,000$$

| Particulars | Calculation | Amount ($) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| Sales | $17,000 \times \$30$ | 510,000 |
| Less: Variable expenses | $17,000 \times \$12$ | 204,000 |
| **Contribution Margin** | $\$510,000 - \$204,000$ | **306,000** |
| Less: Fixed expenses | *Remains Unchanged* | 216,000 |
| **Net Operating Income** | $\$306,000 - \$216,000$ | **90,000** |

$$\mathbf{\$90,000 = \$90,000 \quad \checkmark \text{ (Target Profit Verified!)}}$$

---

## 🛡️ Solution 4: Margin of Safety (MOS)

### A. Margin of Safety in Dollars:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{MOS (Dollars)} &= \text{Total Actual Sales} - \text{B/E Sales} \\
&= \$450,000 - \$360,000 \\
&= \mathbf{\$90,000}
\end{aligned}$$

### B. Margin of Safety Ratio (MSR):
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Margin of Safety Ratio (MSR)} &= \frac{\text{MOS (Dollars)}}{\text{Total Actual Sales}} \times 100 \\
&= \frac{\$90,000}{\$450,000} \times 100 \\
&= \mathbf{20\%}
\end{aligned}$$

---

## 📊 Solution 5: CM Ratio and Revenue Increase Impact

### A. Contribution Margin Ratio (CMR):
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{CM Ratio} &= \frac{\text{CM Per Unit}}{\text{Selling Price Per Unit}} \times 100 \\
&= \frac{\$18}{\$30} \times 100 \\
&= \mathbf{60\%}
\end{aligned}$$

### B. Increase in Net Operating Income:
যেহেতু স্থায়ী খরচ (Fixed Expense) অপরিবর্তিত থাকবে, তাই নতুন যে কোনো বিক্রির পুরো কন্ট্রিবিউশন মার্জিনের অংশটুকুই সরাসরি নিট লাভে রূপান্তরিত হবে।

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Increase in Net Income} &= \text{Increase in Sales} \times \text{CM Ratio} \\
&= \$50,000 \times 60\% \\
&= \mathbf{\$30,000}
\end{aligned}$$
