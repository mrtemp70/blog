
- After preparing the **Trial Balance**, we can prepare the **Financial Statements** and the final table like **Part 1**. But if there are items that need to be reviewed or updated, like **Part 2**, we need to update the Trial Balance by making **Adjusting Entries (সমন্বয় জাবেদা)**.
    
- **Adjusting Entries are usually made at the end of each month** to make sure all income and expenses are recorded correctly. **(প্রতি মাসের শেষে হিসাব ঠিক করার জন্য Adjusting Entries করা হয়।)**
