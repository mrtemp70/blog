## Problem Statement
Engberg company installs lawn sod in home yards. The company's most recent monthly contribution format income statement follows:

| Particulars | Amount ($) | Percent of Sales (%) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Sales | 80,000 | 100% |
| Variable expenses | 32,000 | 40% |
| **Contribution margin** | **48,000** | **60%** |
| Fixed expenses | 38,000 | |
| **Net operating income** | **10,000** | |

## Requirements / Questions
1. Compute the company's degree of operating leverage.
2. Using the degree of operating leverage, estimate the impact on net income of a 5% increase in sales.
3. Verify your estimate from part (2) above by constructing a new contribution format income statement for the company assuming a 5% increase in sales.

---

**Answer:**

## 📐 Solution 1: Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL)

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL)} &= \frac{\text{Contribution Margin}}{\text{Net Operating Income}} \\
&= \frac{\$48,000}{\$10,000} \\
&= \mathbf{4.8}
\end{aligned}$$

---

## 📈 Solution 2: Estimate Impact using DOL

To estimate the percentage change in net operating income without creating a full statement, we use the DOL shortcut formula:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Percentage Change in Profit} &= \text{DOL} \times \text{Percentage Change in Sales} \\
&= 4.8 \times 5\% \\
&= \mathbf{24\%}
\end{aligned}$$

### 💰 Absolute Dollar Impact:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Increase in Net Income} &= \text{Old Net Income} \times 24\% \\
&= \$10,000 \times 0.24 \\
&= \mathbf{\$2,400}
\end{aligned}$$

$$\text{New Expected Net Income} = \$10,000 + \$2,400 = \mathbf{\$12,400}$$

---

## 🗂️ Solution 3: Verification Statement (5% Sales Increase)

Here is the newly constructed contribution format income statement to verify the 24% increase estimate:

| Particulars | Previous Amount ($) | Calculation for New Amount | New Amount ($) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: |
| Sales | 80,000 | $\$80,000 + (\$80,000 \times 5\%)$ | 84,000 |
| Variable expenses | 32,000 | $\$32,000 + (\$32,000 \times 5\%)$ | 33,600 |
| **Contribution Margin** | **48,000** | $\$84,000 - \$33,600$ | **50,400** |
| Fixed expenses | 38,000 | *Remains Unchanged* | 38,000 |
| **Net Operating Income** | **10,000** | $\$50,400 - \$38,000$ | **12,400** |

### 🔍 Verification of Profit Change:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Percentage Change in Profit} &= \frac{\text{New Profit} - \text{Old Profit}}{\text{Old Profit}} \times 100 \\
&= \frac{\$12,400 - \$10,000}{\$10,000} \times 100 \\
&= \frac{\$2,400}{\$10,000} \times 100 \\
&= \mathbf{2 4 \%} \quad \checkmark \text{ (Verified!)}
\end{aligned}$$
