
# Q1: Mr. Rahim's Business Transactions (January 2018)

**Problem Statement:**
Mr. Rahim open a new business in the month of January 2018. The first month of operation the following transaction have been occured.

* **January 1:** Started business with cash 200000 taka.
* **January 2:** Purchased a van taka 60000 in cash.
* **January 5:** Rent paid in cash taka 5000.
* **January 9:** Purchased supplies taka 500 on account.
* **January 10:** Purchased goods taka 50000 in cash.
* **January 15:** Sold taka 15000 in cash.
* **January 20:** Paid sallery taka 1000 in cash.
* **January 25:** Paid insurance premium taka 1200 for one year.
* **January 28:** Sold taka 8000 on credit.
* **January 30:** Paid taka 200 for january (9) transaction.
