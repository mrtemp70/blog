## Goal

Forward all emails from `temp@gmail.com` to `msa@gmail.com`, while keeping the forwarded emails organized under a separate label.

## Step 1: Enable Forwarding on `temp@gmail.com`

1. Sign in to `temp@gmail.com`.
    
2. Go to **Settings → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP**.
    
3. Add `msa@gmail.com` as the forwarding address and complete the verification process.
    
4. Enable forwarding for incoming mail.
    

![[gmail-forward.png]]

---

## Step 2: Organize Forwarded Emails in `msa@gmail.com`

1. Sign in to `msa@gmail.com`.
    
2. Create a new label named `inbox2`.
    
3. Go to **Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses → Create a new filter**.
    
4. Use the following filter:
    

```text
to:(temp@gmail.com)
```

5. When configuring the filter, check the following options:
    
    - ✅ Skip the Inbox (Archive it)
        
    - ✅ Apply the label: `inbox2`
        
    - ✅ Also apply filter to matching conversations (if available)
        
6. Create the filter to save the changes.
    

## Result

All emails sent to `temp@gmail.com` will be automatically forwarded to `msa@gmail.com`, skipped from the main inbox, and organized under the `inbox2` label.
